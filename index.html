Functional Narrative & User Guide

How the prototype is intended to operate.

Purpose

Structive Site Activation is an owner-deployed integration, assurance and acceptance layer between project delivery and long-term operational ownership. Structive provides the methodology and software; the customer retains owner authority and acceptance. It does not replace commissioning or operations source systems. It evaluates whether the operating organization has the capability and authority to accept defined responsibility.

How to use it

Executives start in Command Center and work exceptions. Activation leaders manage constraints and approaching decisions. Functional owners act on obligations assigned to their organization. Assurance users validate evidence and commissioning coverage. Complexity is progressively disclosed through drill-down rather than placed on every screen.

Decision states

Not Eligible, Conditional, Eligible for Acceptance, Accepted and Degraded. Only authorized people accept responsibility or residual risk.

Operations MVP

MVP is both a capability gate and an authority-transfer boundary. It deliberately occurs upstream of full handoff where project conditions allow so Operations can build real facility familiarity while commissioning remains active.

Assurance

L3 uses a 20% field-observation floor; L4/L5 use 25%, with 100% document QA/QC. Risk and findings can increase required sampling. Meeting the floor alone does not prove assurance.

After handoff

Accepted readiness can degrade. Stabilization evaluates whether capability survives actual operations, and Steady State requires sustained evidence rather than elapsed time.
